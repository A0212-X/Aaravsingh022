The problem

Health decisions feel harder when the story is scattered.

Families search symptoms in panic, lose medicine details, and enter short doctor visits without the timeline that matters.

01 One profile for each family member
02 A calmer way to describe symptoms
03 A doctor-ready handoff at the end

What PersonalDoc changes

BeforeNotes across chats and paper
DuringGuided AI conversation
AfterClear next steps + visit prep
AI assists with organization and triage prompts. It does not replace a licensed clinician or emergency services.
The experience

From a worried moment to a clearer conversation.

The workflow is designed around the way families actually care for one another: choose a person, share context, track what changes, and prepare to speak with a physical doctor.

Five simple steps

01 Choose a family member
02 Describe symptoms in English, Hindi, or Hinglish
03 Receive an AI-assisted next step
04 Track vitals and medicines
05 Generate a doctor visit prep sheet
The product

One calm workspace for the whole family.

PersonalDoc combines consultation, blood pressure, blood sugar, child milestones, elderly care, lab explanation, medication adherence, emergency guidance, and doctor handoff preparation.

Built for

HouseholdsManaging family care
CaregiversSupporting parents
PatientsPreparing for visits
DoctorsReceiving a faster handoff
The stack

Small, practical architecture. Ready to grow.

The prototype keeps data local for privacy and uses Gemini only where AI reasoning adds value. The server boundary keeps the API key away from the browser.

AWS path

How AWS can take this prototype to the next stage.

The current build is honest about what is implemented today. AWS can host the existing Node/Express server without pretending that future services are already in production.

Recommended deployment: AWS App Runner for the containerized Node/Express app, AWS Secrets Manager or App Runner secrets for GEMINI_API_KEY, and CloudWatch for logs and health monitoring.
